Hey — I'm Mohammed. I'm an AI/ML Research Engineer based in Dublin, originally from Saudi Arabia. I graduated from University College Dublin in 2024 with a BSc in Computer Science with Data Science, and since then I've been deep in the trenches of building things that actually ship.

My work sits at the intersection of **Reinforcement Learning**, **speech systems**, **multimodal AI**, and **Arabic NLP** — basically, making AI work well in Arabic-first contexts where most existing tools fall short.

## What I'm Building Now

Right now I'm the **Founding Lead Researcher & Engineer at [redec.io](https://redec.io)**. We're building a voice-enabled patient education agent that uses foundation models to simplify lab analysis, post-clinical guidance, and patient follow-ups — across both speech and text. We're currently piloting with 2 clinics and handling 150+ patient interactions weekly. I've been applying RL to fine-tune the conversational policy and building the evaluation framework that feeds into RLHF and preference tuning.

## Things I've Worked On

Before redec, I spent time working on **Arabic TTS adaptation** — designing pipelines that use RL-based Differentiable Reward Optimization (DiffRO) for emotional expressiveness and accent fine-tuning. I also built continuous post-training loops with automated eval gates and embedding-based clustering to surface failure modes and guide retraining. On the LLM side, I've fine-tuned Arabic-first models on domain-specific datasets using instruction tuning and preference tuning, pushing task accuracy significantly on internal benchmarks.

I co-founded **[Taqriry.ai](https://taqriry.ai)**, where I led ML and product engineering for an AI notetaker. We took open-source models and turned them into production-grade transcription, summarization, and automation tools. I built an Arabic ASR + reasoning system with applied RAG, optimizing LLM inference — caching, chunking, streaming — for on-prem deployment. We cut end-to-end latency by 40%. I was also on the sales side: running demos, handling client objections, and closing deals — including with governmental (B2G) entities.

Before that, I was at **IBM** as a Multimodal AI Engineer on the **Watsonx** team. I built and optimized systems that combine ASR with LLM prompt engineering to improve speech and text understanding in enterprise products. I designed evaluation frameworks, ran model validation experiments, and benchmarked internal systems against OpenAI endpoints. Earlier at IBM, as an intern, I engineered a legal chatbot using LLMs that cut customer query handling time by 30% and improved response speed by 25% through better NLP-driven architecture.

## What I Care About

I'm drawn to problems where you need to go from research to production — taking a paper or an idea and turning it into something real that works at scale. I care a lot about **evaluation** (if you can't measure it, you can't improve it), **Arabic-first AI** (the tooling gap is massive), and **RL for language** (RLHF, preference tuning, reward modeling — this is where the field is heading).
